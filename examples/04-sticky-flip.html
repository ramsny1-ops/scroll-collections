Example 04

READ. FLIP. CONTINUE.

The section reserves enough vertical scroll distance for reading while a sticky element rotates according to progress.

Side one

The problem

Fast scroll animations often disappear before the visitor can understand the content. Sticky scroll space decouples reading time from the physical height of the card.

Side two

The explanation

The library maps the reserved section progress to rotation. Use data-flip-turns for rotation count and data-flip-hold for the amount of scroll space.
